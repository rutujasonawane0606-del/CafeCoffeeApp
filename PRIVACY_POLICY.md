# Privacy Policy for Cafe Coffee App

**Last Updated:** October 28, 2026

Welcome to **Cafe Coffee App** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your personal data is collected, used, and shared when you use our mobile application (**Cafe Coffee App**).

---

### 1. Information We Collect
When you use Cafe Coffee App, we may collect the following information to fulfill your coffee orders and improve your user experience:
- **Order Details:** Items selected, cup size, quantities, order date, and total order amounts.
- **Delivery Information:** Delivery address provided by you for order fulfillment.
- **Payment Selection:** Chosen payment method (e.g. Cash on Delivery, UPI / Mobile Banking, Cards). *Note: We do not collect or store your banking PINs, OTPs, or credit card CVV numbers.*
- **App Usage & Diagnostics:** Device model, operating system version, and general usage diagnostics collected anonymously through Google Firebase.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information collected for the following purposes:
- To process, prepare, and deliver your coffee orders.
- To maintain your local order history and favorite items.
- To communicate order status updates ("Preparing", "Out for Delivery", "Delivered").
- To maintain app stability, prevent errors, and optimize performance.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
We use reputable third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:
- **Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore & Analytics):** Used to sync active menu catalogs, banners, and store orders securely in the cloud.
  - [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

---

### 4. Data Storage and Security
- **Local Storage (Room Database):** Your cart, favorites, and past orders are stored locally on your device using encrypted Android SQLite storage.
- **Cloud Storage:** Orders and menu data are transmitted securely via HTTPS/TLS to Google Firebase Firestore.
- We implement commercially acceptable administrative and technical measures to protect your data.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
Our application is not targeted towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

---

### 6. Your Rights
You can clear your local application data or order history at any time through your Android device settings (**Settings > Apps > Cafe Coffee App > Storage > Clear Data**).

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

---
